How We Work

Custom CRM Development Process

1. Business Workflow Analysis

We study how leads and customers move through your business today — including the Excel sheets and WhatsApp groups — and identify exactly where things leak.

2. Architecture Planning

Roles, modules, and integrations are defined up front, and the build is split into phases so the highest-value pieces ship first.

3. Database Design

Leads, customers, activities, users, and permissions are modelled in PostgreSQL with the relationships your reporting will need later.

4. UI/UX

Screens designed for the people who use them daily — sales staff get speed and simplicity, managers get the full picture.

5. Development

Built in Django with reviewable milestones — you see working modules during the build, not a reveal at the end.

6. API Integration

Website forms, WhatsApp, email, and third-party tools are connected and tested with real data flows, not demos.

7. Testing

Role-by-role walkthroughs, permission checks, and edge-case testing — including what happens when two staff touch the same lead.

8. Deployment

Deployed on infrastructure you control, with your data migrated in from Excel or existing tools and verified record by record.

9. Maintenance

Team training at handover, then support plans covering fixes, small enhancements, and backups as your process evolves.

Excel vs Custom CRM

Excel is where most Delhi businesses start — and honestly, for a handful of leads a month, a well-kept sheet is fine. The problems arrive with growth, and they arrive in a predictable order. Here is a straight comparison:

What matters Excel / Sheets Custom CRM
Follow-up remindersManual — depends on someone remembering to checkAutomatic — tasks, nudges, and escalation built in
Multiple staffVersion conflicts, overwrites, no accountabilityRole-based access with every change logged by user
Lead historyScattered across rows, chats, and memoryFull timeline per lead and customer, always current
ReportingManual pivot tables, outdated the moment they're madeLive dashboards: sources, stages, staff performance
IntegrationsCopy-paste between systemsWebsite, WhatsApp, and tools connected via API
Cost shapeFree software, expensive in lost leads and staff timeOne-time build from ₹24,999, no per-seat subscription
