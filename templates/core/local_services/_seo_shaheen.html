Foundations First

Technical SEO

Content can't rank if search engines can't properly crawl, understand, and load your site. Our technical audits cover:

Crawlability

Can Google's crawlers reach and render your important pages without hitting blocks, loops, or dead ends?

Indexation

Are the right pages indexed — and the wrong ones (admin, duplicates, thin utilities) kept out?

Canonicals

One clear canonical per page so similar URLs don't split your ranking signals. (This site implements self-referencing canonicals on every page.)

XML Sitemap

A clean, current sitemap covering indexable pages — and only those — submitted to Search Console.

Robots.txt

Crawler directives that protect private areas without accidentally blocking CSS, JS, or content pages.

Core Web Vitals

Loading speed, interactivity, and layout stability — the experience metrics that affect both rankings and conversions.

Mobile SEO

With mobile-first indexing, your phone experience is your primary experience — tested as such, not adapted afterwards.

HTTPS

Secure serving throughout, including mixed-content checks — browsers warn visitors away from insecure pages.
