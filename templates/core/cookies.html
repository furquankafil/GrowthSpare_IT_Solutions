Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how GrowthSpare IT Solutions utilizes cookies, local browser storage coordinates, and comparable technical trackers to run, analyze, and optimize performance across our web platform and secure workspaces.

1. What are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are tiny textual dataset files placed inside your device browser directories. Local Storage and Session Storage represent built-in browser structures used to save data locally. These tools enable the platform to retain configurations, maintain authenticated user sessions, and gather usage metrics safely.

2. Core Trackers We Deploy

Our web platforms deploy strictly configured categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary trackers: Essential tools protecting session security and CSRF compliance. For instance, storing the `csrftoken` to validate form posts, or cookie sessions to maintain active login profiles on user dashboards. Without these trackers, the workspace platform cannot function.
  • Preference Storage: Local storage parameters used to cache your interface theme choice (Dark Mode or Light Mode configurations).
  • Analytical Performance tracking: Integrates anonymous, aggregated tracking IDs (via Google Analytics) to monitor on-page navigation patterns and page load latency. This helps our engineers optimize Core Web Vitals performance.

3. Customizing Your Settings

Most web browsers enable you to restrict, block, or clear cookies via your browser settings. However, blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in to your GrowthSpare dashboard or completing multi-step consultation bookings.
