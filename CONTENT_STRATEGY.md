# Content Strategy — GrowthSpare IT Solutions

**Cadence:** 2 articles/month, buying-intent first. No thin AI filler. Every article must answer a real question, include examples, link naturally to services, and end with a Free Audit CTA.

**Model fields to fill on every post:** `title`, `slug`, `meta_title` (≤60 chars), `meta_description` (≤155 chars), `category`, `content` (one H1, logical H2/H3), `tags`, `featured_image` (real, relevant, with useful alt).

## Priority 1 — Buying intent (publish first)

### 1. How Much Does a Website Cost in Delhi?
- **Intent:** Comparing quotes, wants realistic ranges.
- **Brief:** Real ranges by type (basic business site from ₹4,999, e-commerce/custom scoped individually). Explain why prices vary: pages, features, content readiness, integrations. What GrowthSpare includes at each tier. Do not invent competitor prices.
- **Structure:** H1 → cost factors → ranges by type → what's included → timeline → FAQ (3–4 real Qs) → CTA Free Audit.
- **Links:** `/services/website-development/`, `/locations/web-development-delhi/`, `/consultation/book/`.
- **Title:** How Much Does a Website Cost in Delhi? (2026 Price Guide)
- **Meta:** Honest website cost ranges in Delhi by project type, what affects pricing, and how to get an accurate quote.

### 2. How to Choose a Website Developer for Your Business
- **Intent:** Evaluation criteria before hiring.
- **Brief:** Genuinely useful checklist applicable to any vendor: portfolio relevance, code ownership, speed/accessibility, SEO basics, support SLA, transparent pricing. Red flags (no contract, page-builder lock-in, guaranteed #1 rankings).
- **Links:** `/services/`, `/locations/`, `/portfolio/`.

### 3. Website vs Instagram for Local Businesses
- **Intent:** "Do I even need a website?" — consideration.
- **Brief:** Where Instagram wins (discovery) vs where a website wins (Google search, ownership, WhatsApp capture, no commission). Examples: restaurant, salon, clinic.
- **Links:** `/industries/small-business-website-development/`, `/services/website-development/`.

### 4. How to Get More WhatsApp Enquiries From a Website
- **Intent:** Transactional how-to.
- **Brief:** Placement (sticky button, hero CTA, contact forms → WhatsApp), click-to-chat copy, Business API vs personal WhatsApp, booking/FAQ automation with human handoff, honest limits.
- **Links:** `/services/ai-whatsapp-automation/`, consultation.

## Writing rules (every article)

- One H1, logical H2/H3, natural keywords, no stuffing.
- 2–3 natural internal links (service + location/industry + consultation).
- FAQ section only if genuinely distinct questions (mirrors FAQPage schema rule).
- CTA: Free Website Audit (+ WhatsApp alternative).
- No fabricated stats, cases, reviews, prices, or client names.
- Fill `meta_title`/`meta_description`; Article schema is automatic via `BlogPostDetailView`.
- Featured image: real/relevant, `alt="<post title>"`, `loading="lazy"`.

## What not to do

- Do not publish 11 thin posts at once. Publish 2 good ones/month.
- Do not target the same keyword with two posts (cannibalisation).
- Do not copy location/industry page text into posts.
